About Gerian Soirée

Music. Reflections. A more thoughtful way of being.

Gerian Soirée

Gerian Soirée is the artistic identity through which I share my music, poetry and reflections with the world. For much of my life, even those closest to me never knew that I could play the piano or compose music. Choosing a pseudonym gave me the freedom to share this part of myself in my own way, without expectation or the need for explanation. Over time, however, I have come to embrace this journey fully.

What began as a quiet space for my creativity has become something more meaningful, an artistic offering shaped by my experiences. It represent my reflections, the music and words that have found their way into my life. Ultimately, it is not about the name behind the work, but about what the work leaves behind.

The Composer

I am a self-taught pianist and composer. My relationship with music developed through listening, experimenting, learning and simply allowing the piano to become a place where ideas could take shape.

The Creative Journey

The journey has never followed a straight line. It has moved through periods of silence, discovery, uncertainty and renewed creativity. Each composition has become part of that journey, carrying its own story and its own reason for existing.

Reflections

Reflections is a collection of ten personal reflections exploring creativity, identity, change, doubt, purpose and the experience of discovering something within yourself later in life.

Beyond the Music

Music is only one part of the story. Writing, photography, spoken reflection and visual storytelling have all become ways of exploring the same questions from different directions.

Connection

At its heart, this project is about connection. A composition begins with an idea, a reflection begins with a question, and a story becomes meaningful when someone else recognises something of themselves within it.

Sometimes the music arrives quietly, long before we understand what it is trying to say.
Perhaps creativity is not about finding something new, but becoming quiet enough to recognise what was already there.

Creating. Reflecting. Connecting.
